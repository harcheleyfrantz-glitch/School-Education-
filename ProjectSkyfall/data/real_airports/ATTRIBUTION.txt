Real airport layouts in this folder were generated by tools/real_airports from open data:

- Runways: OurAirports (https://ourairports.com/data/), public domain.
- Taxiways and their names, gates, aprons, aerodrome boundaries and building footprints:
  (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL),
  obtained through Overture Maps Foundation releases (https://overturemaps.org).
  Some building footprints in Overture come from other open datasets under their own
  open licences.
- Pavement outlines and control tower positions: the FlightGear airport database
  (apt.dat, X-Plane format, from the X-Plane Scenery Gateway), GNU GPL v2.

The derived layout files (*.json) are made available under the same terms as their sources.
